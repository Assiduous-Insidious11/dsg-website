Privacy Policy

Policy version 2026-09-01 · Last updated 2026-09-06

1. What we collect

When you submit a registration, seat request, demo booking or partnership form on this site, we collect your name, company, job title, phone number, email address, the type of enquiry you selected, and any message you choose to write.

2. How we use it

Solely to contact you, confirm your participation, send the event information you requested, and follow up after an event. We do not use this information for unrelated purposes.

3. Retention and access

Lead data is restricted: only event and marketing roles can access it, access requires an account and is logged. Records are kept for 24 months from your last interaction, then deleted or anonymised.

4. Your rights

You may request access, correction, export or deletion of your personal data, and withdraw consent at any time. Contact us below; we respond within 15 working days.

5. Automated protection

Forms use automated anti-spam controls (hidden field, submission-speed check, rate limiting). Submissions classified as bots are not recorded as valid leads.
