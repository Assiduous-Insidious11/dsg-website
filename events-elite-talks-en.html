Elite Six Talks

Elite Six Talks: go deeper on the problem, make the connection real.

Elite Six Talks are closed-door sessions of 6 to 15 people, built for CIOs, IT heads, procurement and supply chain leaders and business decision-makers working through the same set of real problems.

6–15Closed-door seats
ThursdaysRegular cadence
4Closed-door cases documented
Format

Less scale, more problem density and better company at the table.

Each session is built around one industry or business topic: it opens with a case and a question, then works through peer questioning, experience exchange and an action list that turns into follow-up opportunities.

Curated invitations

Participants are screened by industry, function and topic so the table can hold a real conversation.

Closed-door depth

No broad keynote: the focus is the real friction in organisation, systems, data, AI adoption and supply chain practice.

Follow-up connection

Participants on the same topic continue the conversation in later sessions; cross-company introductions are arranged by the event team.
